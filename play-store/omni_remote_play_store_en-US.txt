Full description:
Your phone. Your dashboard. Your IoT projects under control. 🚀

Tired of rigid apps that never fit your project? Omni Remote is the fastest way to control your Arduino, ESP8266, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi over MQTT — with a dashboard YOU design in under a minute.

No account. No cloud lock-in. No coding on the app side. Just connect, add a device and start controlling.

⚡ SET UP IN 4 SIMPLE STEPS
1. Enter your MQTT broker (HiveMQ, Mosquitto, EMQX or your own).
2. Create a group, like "Living Room" or "Robot".
3. Add a device — Omni Remote instantly gives you the Command and Status topics.
4. Copy the topics into your Arduino/ESP code. Done — control it from anywhere! 🌍

🎛️ EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO CONTROL YOUR HARDWARE
• Switches (ON/OFF): lights, relays, outlets, pumps, locks.
• Sliders & numeric controls: LED brightness (PWM), motor speed, servo angles, thermostats. Set your own min, max and step.
• Live status: see the real state of your device, with a connected/disconnected indicator.
• Smart groups: organize by room, project or robot, each with its own name and icon.

🔗 FLEXIBLE & SECURE MQTT CONNECTION
• Works with any local or cloud MQTT broker.
• Username & password authentication.
• Topics auto-generated and ready to copy — less time configuring, more time building.

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
• Hundreds of icons for your devices and groups.
• Light & Dark themes, and a color palette to match your style.
• Available in English and Spanish.
• Modern, smooth interface built with Flutter.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
All your settings and devices are stored locally on your phone. We don't ask for accounts and we don't use proprietary servers.

Makers • Students • Home automation fans • Robotics projects • School science fairs • Smart home DIY
